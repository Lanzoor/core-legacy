About

core-legacy is an archive containing previous works related to core and Lanzaforge that are no longer actively maintained.
It exists to preserve the history, evolution, and development of the project over time.

How We Recover and Preserve Works

α. Recovering the works

We usually recover or preserve works by revisiting the history of our Discord messages, GitHub commits, and general OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) research.

These sources allow us to reconstruct previous versions of works, track changes made over time, and recover content that may no longer exist in its original location. GitHub commits provide a detailed history of project changes, while archived conversations and other publicly available sources help provide additional context behind each version.

The goal of this process is not to restore outdated works as active content, but rather to preserve them as a record of the project's development, decisions, and evolution over time.

β. Preserving the works

After a work has been successfully recovered, it will be ported into the according format if needed, archived here and preserved for future reference.

Once the preservation process is complete, the archived works will not be modified unless a significant issue is discovered, such as incorrect data, corruption, or other problems that affect the accuracy or integrity of the archive.
